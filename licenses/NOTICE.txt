GLSL Support bundles glslang 16.6.0, SPIRV-Tools and SPIRV-Headers from the Khronos Group.
Their license and contributor notices are in glslang.txt, spirv-tools.txt and spirv-headers.txt.
Native source revisions and archive digests are recorded in native/CMakeLists.txt.

The offline GLSL catalog and legacy editor regression shaders derive from the glsl_analyzer project.
See glsl-analyzer-catalog.txt.

The published OpenGL 4.x XHTML comes from https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenGL-Refpages/tree/main/gl4/html.
The original page notices are collected in opengl-reference-pages.txt. LicenseRef-FreeB.txt and
OPUBL-1.0.txt contain the corresponding license texts. GLSL Support supplies additional OpenGL 4.6
entries from the Khronos specifications. The downloadable OpenGL and GLSL specifications and the
ARB_clip_control extension retain their original notices.

MathJax 2.7.9 renders the reference equations. Its Apache 2.0 license is in mathjax.txt.
The font directories in reference_pages/mathjax/fonts retain their individual license files.
